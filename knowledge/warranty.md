# Warranty and repairs

## Warranty length
All Nova Gear products come with a 2 year warranty that covers manufacturing defects. The warranty starts on the delivery date and is tied to the order, so keep your order confirmation.

## What is covered
Battery that drops below 80 percent of its capacity within the first year, charging problems, speaker or microphone failures, and screen defects are covered. Water damage on products without an IP rating, drops, and cracked screens are not covered.

## Making a warranty claim
Describe the problem to the support assistant and give your order number. If the problem looks like a defect, the assistant will pass your case to a human agent, who will send a repair or replacement label. Repairs take 7 to 10 business days. If the product cannot be repaired we send a replacement of the same or a better model.

## Battery care
For the longest battery life keep the device between 20 and 80 percent charge when you can, and avoid leaving it in hot places such as a parked car.
