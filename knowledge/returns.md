# Returns and refunds

## Return window
You can return any unused product within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. The product must be in its original packaging with all accessories. Returns are free for customers in the EU. Outside the EU the customer pays the return shipping.

## How to start a return
Tell the support assistant your order number and the reason for the return. It will register the return and send you a prepaid label by email within one business day. Alternatively, email returns@novagear.example.

## Refund timing
Refunds go back to the original payment method. After the parcel arrives at our warehouse it is inspected within 2 business days, and the refund is issued right after. Banks usually need 3 to 7 business days to show the money.

## Items that cannot be returned
Opened earbuds for hygiene reasons (unless defective), gift cards and products damaged by the customer cannot be returned.

## Exchanges
We do not do direct exchanges. Return the item for a refund and place a new order. This is the fastest way to get the replacement.
