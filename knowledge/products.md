# Product help

## Pairing Pulse Buds
Open the case with the buds inside and hold the button on the back for 3 seconds until the light blinks white. Then choose Pulse Buds in the Bluetooth settings of your phone. To reset them, hold the button for 10 seconds until the light blinks red.

## Noise cancelling modes
Press and hold the touch area on either bud to switch between noise cancelling, transparency and off. The Pro model also adapts the level automatically to your surroundings.

## Charging Orbit Watch and Orbit Band
Use the magnetic charger that comes in the box. A full charge takes about 90 minutes for the watch and 60 minutes for the band. If the device does not wake up, leave it on the charger for 15 minutes first.

## Volt Bank on flights
The Volt Bank 20K is below the 100 Wh airline limit and can be carried in hand luggage. Power banks must not be placed in checked luggage.

## Waterproofing
The Echo Mini is IP67: it survives 1 metre of water for 30 minutes. The Orbit Watch is rated for swimming up to 50 metres. The Pulse Buds are sweat and splash resistant but not for swimming.

## Software updates
Firmware for earbuds, watch and speakers is updated through the free Nova Gear app. Keep Bluetooth on and the device within reach while updating.
