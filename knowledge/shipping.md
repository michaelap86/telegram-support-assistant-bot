# Shipping

## Delivery times and costs
Nova Gear ships from a warehouse in Rotterdam. We deliver to all 27 EU countries, for example Germany, Austria, France, Spain, Italy and the Netherlands. Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the EU and costs 4.90 EUR. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 9.90 EUR. Orders over 60 EUR ship free with standard delivery.

## International shipping
We ship to the UK, Switzerland, Norway, the USA and Canada. International delivery takes 5 to 10 business days. Customs duties and import taxes are paid by the customer and are not included in the order total.

## Order processing time
Orders placed before 14:00 CET on a business day are packed the same day. Orders placed later, or at the weekend, are packed the next business day. You get an email with the tracking number as soon as the parcel is handed to the carrier.

## Tracking a parcel
Every parcel has a tracking number from DHL or UPS. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear after the shipping email. If tracking has not moved for 5 business days, contact support and we will open an investigation with the carrier.

## Changing the delivery address
The address can be changed only while the order status is "processing". After the parcel has shipped we cannot change it, but you can redirect it through the carrier's website using the tracking number.
